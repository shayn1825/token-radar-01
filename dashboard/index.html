How the ranking works

implied value = TVL × comp × growth × age × breadth

comp — what the market pays per TVL dollar

For each sector, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the protocols in that sector that already have a token (at least four comparables, shrunk toward the global median). Sectors with fewer comparables use the global median. The table below shows every multiple this board used.

SectorMultipleComps

growth · age · breadth

  • growth — 1 plus the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+60%. A protocol gaining TVL is worth more than one losing it.
  • age — 0.50 rising to 2.00 over a protocol’s first three years running with no token recorded. Longer with no token, more pent-up launch value: a three-year veteran is worth double its TVL multiple, a brand-new protocol half.
  • breadth — +0.03 per chain after the first, up to five extra chains. More chains, more surface to farm.

What “no token” means

A protocol records no token when DefiLlama records none for it. That is the snapshot’s record, not proof it has none. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out, as are versioned names (V2, 2.0) and exchange products.

Read this before farming

Implied values are this board’s own claim, never a prediction that a token will launch, and never financial advice. Farm plans say what each step costs and how long it takes; they cannot promise a reward. Never deposit money you need back within a month, and never share a seed phrase with any site.
